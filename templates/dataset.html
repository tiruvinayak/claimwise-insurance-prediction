Explicit Workflow for Training on a New Dataset

Uploading a dataset never automatically retrains the saved production model. To train models on a new dataset, execute the dedicated pipeline workflow:

  1. Data health validation: audit missing cells, remove duplicates, inspect frequencies.
  2. Feature preprocessing: fit encoders and scalers on training splits only.
  3. Partitioning: 80/20 split with fixed random seed (42).
  4. Model training & CV: fit all candidates with cross-validation.
  5. Evaluation & verification: measure MAE, MSE, RMSE and R² on held-out data.
  6. Artifact preservation: save models into Models/ and metrics into Outputs/.

Script references: Src/claimwise_preprocessing_pipeline.py & Src/train_models.py.
