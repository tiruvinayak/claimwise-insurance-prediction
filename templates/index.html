How ClaimWise Works

A structured six-step workflow designed to transform raw insurance records into ML-ready features.

01

DATA

Ingests 50,000 raw insurance claims records.

02

CLEAN

Drops id column and handles missing values.

03

ENCODE

Applies LabelEncoder to text categories.

04

SCALE

Normalizes continuous variables via StandardScaler.

05

TRAIN

Fits regression algorithms on 40,000 training rows.

06

PREDICT

Estimates continuous insurance claim loss values.

Input Features

The model consumes the 130 input columns of the dataset, named exactly as in Dataset/01_raw_claimwise_50000.csv:

🔢

CONTINUOUS (cont1–cont14)

14 numeric measurements, standardized with StandardScaler fitted on the 40,000 training rows only.

🏷️

BINARY CATEGORICAL

73 of the 116 cat1–cat116 columns have exactly two possible values and are label-encoded to integers.

🏷️

MULTI-LEVEL CATEGORICAL

The remaining 43 categorical columns hold 3 to 266 levels each, also label-encoded — no category is invented or renamed.

🎯

TARGET (loss)

The continuous claim loss to predict: 10.00 to 85,923.56 in the raw dataset, mean 3035.01. The unit is not defined by the dataset.
