How ClaimWise Works

A rigorous, reproducible pipeline transforming raw insurance claims into validated regression predictions.

01

INGEST

Audits 50,000 raw claims across 132 columns for missing cells, duplicates, and column types.

02

CLEAN

Drops record identifier id and handles imputation without synthetic bias.

03

ENCODE

Applies LabelEncoder to 116 categorical columns (73 binary, 43 multi-level).

04

SCALE

Standardizes 14 continuous variables with StandardScaler fitted solely on training rows.

05

TRAIN

Trains 13 regression models with cross-validation and selects winner by lowest test RMSE.

06

PREDICT

Provides validated claim loss estimates through the saved preprocessor and best model.
