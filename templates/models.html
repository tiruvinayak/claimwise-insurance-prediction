Regression Metric Definitions & Evaluation Criteria

Because the primary target loss is continuous ($10.00 to $85,923.56), regression performance metrics are strictly reported. Note that R² represents the proportion of variance explained and is not a percentage accuracy:

MAE (Mean Absolute Error)

The average magnitude of absolute prediction errors across claims: \(\frac{1}{n} \sum |y_i - \hat{y}_i|\).

Lower is better (dollar scale).

MSE (Mean Squared Error)

Average of squared residuals: \(\frac{1}{n} \sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2\). Quantifies variance of prediction errors.

Lower is better (variance in $²).

RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)

Square root of mean squared errors: \(\sqrt{\frac{1}{n} \sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}\). Primary benchmark metric penalizing large errors.

Lower is better (production model selector).

R² (Coefficient of Determination)

Proportion of variance in claim loss explained by the model relative to the mean prediction baseline: \(1 - \frac{\text{SS}_{\text{res}}}{\text{SS}_{\text{tot}}}\).

Higher is better (variance explained).

Median AE (Median Absolute Error)

Median of absolute residuals: \(\text{median}(|y_i - \hat{y}_i|)\). Robust to extreme catastrophic claim outliers.

Lower is better (typical claim error).

DATA INTEGRITY SAFEGUARD

Data Leakage Prevention Protocol

Imputation and deduplication were no-ops — the raw dataset contains 0 missing values and 0 duplicate rows. Label encoding is a target-free vocabulary mapping (no target information is used). The StandardScaler was originally fitted on the 40,000-row training partition of the seed-42 split; when wave 3 re-partitioned the preprocessed matrix, a portion of those scaler-fitted rows now fall inside the new holdout. This is a documented, minor limitation that affects only scale-sensitive (linear) models. The production champion — a tree-based CatBoost model — is invariant to feature scaling, so this limitation has no effect on its predictions or on the reported champion metrics.
